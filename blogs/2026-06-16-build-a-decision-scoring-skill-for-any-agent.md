The /decide skill is a five-step pipeline (discovery, research, assessment, risk evaluation, and mechanical scoring) for making and documenting technical decisions with AI agents. Risk ratings map to fixed confidence values across weighted pillars (Trust, Reliability, Operational Excellence, Resource Optimization, Cost Optimization, Fairness), and the output is a versioned Architectural Decision Record in markdown. The pattern is transferable across different agents and organizations to turn coding agents into guardrails for engineering design.
